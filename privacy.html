Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026 · Governed by the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how Coldberry Ice Cream Nigeria ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards personal data when you visit our website (coldberry.ng) or place ice cream delivery and pickup requests through our ordering portal. We operate retail ice cream parlors in Abuja (Garki, Lugbe, Gwarinpa) and Lagos (Admiralty Way Lekki, Yaba, Surulere, Okota, Festac, Ajao).

If you have questions regarding this policy or your personal data, reach our Data Protection liaison at privacy@coldberry.ng or via phone at +234 905 530 3848 (09055303848).

2. What Personal Data We Collect

We minimize data collection strictly to what is necessary to fulfill orders:

  • Contact Details: Your full name and telephone / WhatsApp phone number.
  • Delivery Information: Delivery street address, apartment / estate details, and landmarks (collected only when Delivery is chosen).
  • Order Details: Selected ice cream tubs, waffle bowls, toppings, sides, beverages, quantities, optional dietary/allergen notes, and chosen outlet location.
  • Browser Storage Preferences: Locally stored flags on your device (coldberry-theme for dark/light display preference, and coldberry_cart for temporary items stored in your browser cart).

Payment Data: We do NOT collect, transmit, or store debit card details, bank PINs, or CVV codes on our website. Orders are confirmed and settled directly via parlor WhatsApp channels, bank transfer to verified company accounts, or Point of Sale (POS) terminal on delivery / pickup.

3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Processing

Under Section 25 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, we process personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Performance of a Contract: Processing your name, telephone number, address, and product selection is necessary to prepare, confirm, and deliver your order.
  • Legitimate Interest: Maintaining operational security, preventing fraud or duplicate orders, and improving parlor dispatching efficiency.
  • Consent: When you submit the checkout form and tick the agreement checkbox before launching WhatsApp order communication.

4. Third-Party Disclosures & Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose order information only to necessary service providers:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): When you submit your order, your prefilled order items and contact details are handed over to WhatsApp so you can chat directly with store dispatchers.
  • Courier & Delivery Drivers: Your name, phone number, and delivery address are shared with our logistics personnel solely for the duration of the delivery run.
  • CDN Providers: Fast content delivery networks (cdnjs) serve public icons and styling scripts. Your IP address may be logged in web server transit logs as part of standard internet communication.

5. Cookies & Browser Storage

Our website operates cleanly without third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or invasive analytics pixels. We utilize standard client browser storage exclusively for strictly necessary functionality:

Storage Key Type Purpose Duration
coldberry-theme localStorage Remembers your visual theme preference (light or dark mode) Persistent until cleared
coldberry_cart localStorage Stores your active ice cream and drink selections across pages Session / until cleared
coldberry-icecream-cart localStorage Legacy compatibility store for older order cart integrations Session / until cleared
coldberry_user_session HttpOnly Cookie (Strictly Necessary) Keeps you signed into your Coldberry Ice Cream customer account and surfaces order & refund notifications 7 days (or until Sign Out)
coldberry_customer_name / coldberry_active_pins localStorage Remembers your recent order PIN and name for quick tracking and feedback auto-fill Persistent until cleared

6. Data Retention & Security

Digital order logs and messages are retained for a maximum of 12 months for bookkeeping, reconciliation, and customer service dispute resolution, after which they are purged. We apply industry-standard HTTPS encryption across all web traffic to guard against interception.

7. Your Rights Under the NDPA

As a data subject in Nigeria, you have specific rights under the NDPA:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete contact records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data where retention is no longer necessary.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: If you believe your data has been handled improperly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@coldberry.ng.

8. Dietary & Allergen Notes, Dispute Resolution & Class Action Waiver

If you voluntarily include allergy, dietary, or Halal preferences in your order notes or customer communications, we process that information strictly to transmit your request to the fulfilling parlor branch and never sell or use it for automated profiling. Complete legally checked SKU allergen, cross-contact, and Halal disclosures (including Gummy Bears Topping, Candy-Coated Chocolate Gems Topping, Rainbow Sprinkles Topping, nut, dairy, soy, and wheat SKUs) are governed by Section 7 of our Terms & Conditions.

Individual Claims & Class Action Waiver: Subject to any non-waivable statutory right to lodge an individual complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) under the NDPA 2023, and to the fullest extent permitted by applicable Nigerian law, any civil claim, dispute, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Privacy Policy, our data practices, or any allergen/dietary disclosure must be brought solely in your individual capacity and NOT as a plaintiff, representative, or class member in any purported class action, collective action, consolidated action, or representative lawsuit, in accordance with Section 9 of our Terms & Conditions.

Notice: This policy outlines Coldberry Ice Cream Nigeria operational data practices and dispute terms. It does not constitute formal legal counsel. For high-stakes regulatory inquiries, consult a qualified Nigerian legal practitioner.
